# Calendar identities and broader sports coverage - v1.0.17

The same contest can now combine English/Arabic participant names, published slug aliases and reviewed team abbreviations into one card. Both participants, sport, squad qualifiers and a nearby kickoff must agree. No substring or edit-distance guesses are used. Cross-provider home/away reversal preserves broadcast links and swaps simple numeric scores and crests; complex reversed scores are omitted rather than attached to the wrong participant.

Same-ID entries also retain their watch options rather than dropping later records. Reviewed duplicate race titles from separate providers can merge within ten minutes; practice numbers and other session identities remain distinct. Men/women/youth competitions and different dates remain separate. Identical short city names need an explicit NBA/WNBA/NHL/MLB/NFL context; ambiguous city names cannot pick a team. Saved matches merge too, and can be unsaved through an equivalent listing from a different provider.

The calendar now fetches ESPN all-football, NBA and NFL listings alongside existing beIN, Yacine, VIPRow, StreamCenter, Livekora, Yalla, FilGoal, F1, NHL, MLB and Raceweek sources. A beIN assignment is not required to show a fixture or retain its exact published web player. Claimed channel labels on third-party cards are not promoted to verified catalog assignments. On the current check Livekora returned an empty dated schedule and q.korahlive.online failed DNS; no guessed replacement domain was added. Sampled Yalla fixture pages had no static embedded player. The app retains its resolver and reports unavailable sources rather than claiming a live feed from the schedule alone.

Logo coverage: 1018 indexed image records from ESPN and the current-season football-logos collection, plus images published by fixture providers. Includes football, NBA/WNBA, NFL, NHL, MLB and rugby. Every sport filter has category artwork; unrecognized participants retain initials. See SPORTS-LOGO-SOURCES.md for source links and scope.

Validation: 61 JVM tests and Android instrumentation (imports, persistence, multilingual favorite equivalence, six-sport logo lookup, league-specific logos and every sport icon). The October 29 emulator calendar snapshot returned 315 events with no source errors: 100 primary ESPN Football listings, 116 web-enabled fixtures and 182 fixtures with a home crest. No remaining duplicate pair matched the identity rules. Counts are snapshots; unknown aliases and ambiguous team identities cannot be guaranteed deduplicated. A prior snapshot before adding league-specific city aliases had 317 events. Name/squad normalization uses bounded caches to avoid repeated regex work in dense calendars.

This release does not certify every upstream video server or change the beIN buffering recovery from v1.0.16.

An observed beIN/VIPRow/ESPN Adelaide fixture also prompted country-suffix handling and the reviewed Lion City Sailors / Home United alias. The [club documents its former name](https://www.lioncitysailorsfc.sg/the-club/). Country conflicts remain separate; U21 and other squad markers are retained. An Android regression check covers the observed provider names and ten-minute kickoff difference. The logo index initializes on a background thread and refreshes visible cards after loading.
