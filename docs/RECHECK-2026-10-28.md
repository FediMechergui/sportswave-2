## Provider findings

- The current configured Yacine endpoint `def67.ycnapi.com` fails DNS resolution locally on repeated checks. No channel session can be refreshed through that endpoint on this connection. This is an upstream availability observation, not proof of permanent shutdown or a global outage.
- Checked all 43 non-sports bundled replacements: 41 delivered HLS segments; Samarra returned HTTP 500 and Oman TV returned non-media content. These transport results alone do not certify decoded playback, channel identity, or current live programming. See `CHANNEL-RECHECK-2026-10-28.csv`.
- Reference app Streamed `/api/matches/all` redirected to its HTML homepage, not the expected JSON collection. KorahLive failed to load locally.
- Both reference app channel pages redirected from `pl.koralive1.cc` to `pl.matchlivehd.com`. Checked the published Arryadia and beIN 4 embedded players in a browser.
- Arryadia: decoded picture displayed the Arryadia/3 HD mark, and its playback position advanced by about five seconds over a five-second check. This does not establish that a particular scheduled event was airing.
- beIN 4: first attempt played a 58-second football promotional clip with KORA TV branding, not a verified beIN 4 channel feed. Second attempt failed with `hls:networkError_fragLoadError`. Rejected as a replacement; no captured media sessions or signatures were incorporated.
- Official beIN MENA channel guide still responded with 40 channel records. Guide availability does not imply video availability.

## App changes in 1.0.12

- All 49 bundled public alternatives are now discoverable without a successful Yacine catalogue sync. Retained names, exact IDs and categories from the previously captured catalogue; no expiring playback sessions are bundled. Remote discovery can still replace metadata for the same ID.
- Six built-in Arabic sports sources now participate in the existing exact-channel provider recovery path if their public feed fails.
- Calendar merging now preserves home/away order. A reversed fixture cannot donate its score or broadcast assignments to another fixture. Races still require explicit broadcast references rather than inheriting a football channel.
- Added regression coverage for catalogue availability during an API outage, reversed participants, and separation of numbered beIN, XTRA, MAX, English, French and AFC feeds.
- Fixed Asian/African Champions League and CAF Confederation Cup programmes incorrectly falling into Other instead of Football.

Bundesliga returned zero for both tested dates. OpenF1 returned 22 positions for the completed 25 October United States race; this was not a live race test.

## Limits

No verified replacement for the unavailable Yacine backend, Arabic beIN 9, Arabic XTRA, or Arabic MAX was found in this reference app. A rendered webpage, promotional clip, or advancing video clock alone is not evidence of the correct live sports feed. No claim is made that the full remote catalogue works.
